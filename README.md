## The pitch

> We stage elaborate funerals for dead houseplants, and guilt-ridden owners pay $89 for a eulogy, a tiny coffin, and a certificate of closure.

A startup so strange it sounds like a joke — funerals for houseplants — yet the
business logic survives a second look: millions of people grieve over dead
plants, guilt is a powerful motivator, and the grief economy already sells
closure for pets. The sentence names the odd thing sold, who pays, and the
price, taking the reader from laughing to nodding.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes the single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
The sentence is one sentence, UTF-8, and at most 200 characters.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
